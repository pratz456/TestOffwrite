## Findings and recommendation
1. Safety is where round 2 wanted it: 0 invariant violations in 8 scorecards (round 2: 6), 0 wrong deductions beyond the synthetic Verizon twin, 1 rejection in 824 requests (round 2: 11), never-approve assertion green for all three models. Every remaining status disagreement is a question or a nondeductible/blocked answer, not an approval.
2. **P1 (gate) — the off-category-citation gate discards gpt-4.1-mini's correct vehicle approvals.** It cites `business-162` on all 8 `vehicle_expense` approvals per run while `CATEGORY_EVIDENCE.vehicle_expense = ['travel-463','mileage-rates']` (`lib/ai/transaction-tax-policy.ts:114`), so `:372` replaces the answer with the generic purpose question: 2 of 26 descriptor approvals and 1 corpus approval lost per run (`parking`, `ezpass`, `ezpass-rideshare-tolls`), and GEICO/Shell/Uber-trip get a generic question instead of the vehicle-method one. Append `business-162` to the vehicle_expense list (keep `travel-463` first so the category rule is unchanged): parking/tolls then approve through `:475`, insurance and fuel fall into the `vehicle_method` gate at `:490`. This alone brings gpt-4.1-mini to 26/26 descriptors and 20/28 corpus, level with gpt-4o.
3. **P1 (gate) — `proposed_purpose` reaches the user in 3 of 258 eligible results.** It is attached only when the model itself returned `is_deductible: true` on a purpose-less merchant (`:457-461`); all three models now self-flag `needs_more_info` first, so the one-tap proposal is skipped and the model's free-text question is shown. Attach `proposed_purpose` + `proposedPurposeQuestion` whenever `missing_fields` includes `business_purpose` and the merchant is `business_likely`/high with a `defaultPurpose`, regardless of the model's `is_deductible`.
4. **Prompt — merchant intelligence outranks explicit personal notes for gpt-4.1-mini.** `zoom-personal-use` ("Family video calls, personal plan"), `starbucks-alone-personal` ("My morning coffee") and `uber-personal-ride` ("Ride home from a concert") were ok/personal in round 2 and are needs_more_info with merchant-default questions in both round-3 runs (gpt-4o repeats the Starbucks one). Rule 4 says "do not request a business purpose contrary to an explicit personal purpose" only in the groceries example; state it for every merchant and say that an explicit personal note outranks `merchant_intelligence.disposition`.
5. Facts saved in `note` rather than `business_purpose` are re-asked by all three models (`venmo-contractor-payment` "Paid contractor Alex for logo illustration work", `bank-fee` "Business checking monthly fee" — both approved by gpt-4o/gpt-5.4-mini in round 2), and saved percentages still are (Costco 30%: gpt-4.1-mini/gpt-5.4-mini; TurboTax 60%: gpt-4o).
