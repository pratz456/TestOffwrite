## Findings
1. **P1 — the round-3 finding-2 fix does not reach users: every `vehicle_expense` approval is downgraded at persistence.** `lib/ai/transaction-tax-policy.ts:488-492` approves business-trip parking/tolls and forces `result.category = 'vehicle_expense'` (comment at `:254`: "need no method review"); `lib/ai/analysis-persistence.ts:98-104` then treats any approval whose review category maps to a `_REVIEW_REQUIRED` recorded category (`vehicle_expense` → `VEHICLE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `lib/transactions/ai-review-contract.ts:8`; also `equipment`, `home_office`, `other`) or whose `deductiblePercent` is not 100 (50 for meals_50) as `needs_more_info` with `isDeductible: null`. Expected (per policy and the harness): ParkMobile / E-ZPass with a saved client-visit purpose → `ok`, deductible, vehicle_expense. Observed on the persisted doc (Step B `e2e-parking`, `e2e-ezpass`; probe: 9 of 46 approvals): `ai_suggestion.status = needs_more_info`, `isDeductible = null`, `deductionStatus = "Needs more information"`, question "Confirm the business use and applicable tax treatment before claiming a deduction." while `ai_customized_reason` still says "…ordinary vehicle expenses deductible on Schedule C line 9" and `ai_explanation.headline` says "Needs one fact: what was bought and its business or personal purpose" (`lib/ai/explanation.ts:181`) directly above "Purpose you saved: Parking at the client's office…". Either exempt parking/tolls (and full-percentage `other`/insurance approvals) from the persistence downgrade, or make the harness score the persisted suggestion so approvable_approved measures what users see (26/26 descriptors at the engine is 22/26 persisted).
2. **Harness/production gap for partial percentages.** Approvals at a saved business-use percentage (40/60/70%) are `ok` in the harness and `needs_more_info` after persistence because the review contract has no partial-percent confirmation (`analysis-persistence.ts:100-101`). If that is intended, the persisted question should say so ("Confirm the 40% business share…") instead of the generic sentence, and the evaluation should not count these as approvals.
3. **Finding 4 (round 3) is two-thirds fixed.** `zoom-personal-use` and `uber-personal-ride` are ok/personal; `starbucks-alone-personal` ("My morning coffee") still gets "Who was at this meal and what business was discussed?": the note matches neither the prompt's Rule-4 examples nor `EXPLICIT_PERSONAL_NOTE` (`lib/ai/transaction-tax-policy.ts:263`, applied only to model approvals at `:379`), and the model asked rather than approved, so the server gate never runs.
4. Emulator-only: a trigger that fires before the worker origin is listening is lost (`fetch failed` → function killed, no emulator retry). Production relies on `retry: true` (`functions-analysis/src/index.ts:7`); the demo could seed after `next dev` is ready or re-enqueue with `enqueueAccountAnalysis`, but this is not an application bug.
5. Step B expectation mismatch, not a bug: Amazon has no `proposedPurpose` because its merchant entry is `needs_purpose` without a `defaultPurpose` (`lib/ai/merchant-intelligence.ts:296`); the path works for `business_likely`/high merchants (`e2e-adobe-nopurpose` persisted "Design and creative software subscription used for client work").
6. Label debt unchanged from round 3 (finding 8): 2 new Rule-4 ok/personal answers (`zara-designer-no-note`, `great-clips-stylist`) and the same blocked/personal groups account for the 23 corpus status disagreements; none is an approval.

**Verdict: yes — AI analysis is being sent to GPT and working.** The configured key reaches OpenAI (200, 132 models), and a newly posted transaction travels Firestore trigger → worker `enqueue` → `analysis_tasks` → trigger → worker `process` → `gpt-4.1-mini-2025-04-14` → `ai_suggestion` (policy `federal-transactions-2026-09-18.1`) in 1.9–3.2 s for 8/8 new records and 13/13 pre-seeded records that reached the worker, with zero provider failures across 275 paid requests (206 eval + 47 probe + 22 demo), both vitest live tests green and no never-approve violation. The engine improved on its own targets (status 110/133 and 70/73, 26/26 approvable descriptors), but the persistence layer withholds the parking/toll and partial-percentage approvals the engine now makes (Findings 1–2).
