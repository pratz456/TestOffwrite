## Findings and recommendation
1. Over-asking is fixed for three models and worse for gpt-4o-mini. gpt-4o/gpt-4.1-mini/gpt-5.4-mini approve 10/11/9 of 16 corpus and 22/23/19 of 26 descriptor approvable cases (round 1: 0/3/— of 16); their remaining questions are Rule-2 facts (placed-in-service, attendees, percentage). gpt-4o-mini approved 0/16 and 5/26: it copies the Rule-3 "Confirm the purpose" template onto transactions whose purpose is saved and keeps asking "exclusively" (28 of its 37 over-asks) — it does not follow the ordered rules. Drop it as the `transaction` model.
2. **P1 — approvals under category `other` bypass every server gate.** New wrong deductions: GEICO auto insurance for an 80%-business rideshare car at 100% (gpt-4o-mini, gpt-4.1-mini; Rule 1's "business insurance" clause), H&R Block "1040 and Schedule C" prep fee at 100% (gpt-4.1-mini, gpt-5.4-mini; only the Schedule C share is a business expense), PARKMOBILE $6.50 at a closing (gpt-4o, gpt-4.1-mini; arguably right, but it skipped the vehicle gate). Fix server-side regardless of model: an `other`/`is_deductible=true` result whose text matches auto-insurance/vehicle words → vehicle_method question; tax-preparation merchants → business-portion question; Rule 1 should read "business insurance other than auto and health". These are the only invariant violations in round 2 (0 in the corpus).
3. Apart from gpt-5.4-mini's one by-design percent-mismatch rejection, every remaining `AI_INVALID_OUTPUT` has a single cause: `evidence_ids` empty or null (gpt-4o-mini 7/146, gpt-4o 3/146, gpt-4.1-mini 0). It destroys correct answers — the Rule-5 blocks of IRS USATAXPYMT and FRANCHISE TAX BOARD (gpt-4o-mini and gpt-4o both) and gpt-4o's Blue Shield block — because `groundTransactionAnalysis` rejects `!ids.length` before the tax-authority gate can run. Treat a missing citation on `blocked`/`needs_more_info` like an off-category one (substitute the server's applicable ids); the IRS/FTB descriptor cases then land on the hard block as designed (gpt-4.1-mini/gpt-5.4-mini already do: `blocked`, category `other`).
4. Grounding false positives cost approvals: `HOME_RENT_PATTERN` is tested against the model's own explanation, so gpt-5.4-mini's "rather than home-office costs" turned two correct WeWork rent approvals into the home-office question (studio-rent-separate-location, wework); `CLUB_DUES_PATTERN` re-asked the trainer whose purpose already says "Floor space rental to train my clients" (all four models); saved 40%/70% percentages were re-asked by gpt-4o-mini/gpt-4o/gpt-5.4-mini because Rule 2 never says a saved percentage is sufficient. Limit the rent/club patterns to saved context + merchant, and add "a saved business_use_percentage is the allocation; return it as deductible_percent".
5. Durable items under $500 (desk $249, monitor $349.99): all four models chose `equipment` and asked placed-in-service facts, contrary to Rule 2's $500 floor and the corpus/descriptor labels (supplies, ok). State the floor in Rule 1 ("a durable item at or under $500 is supplies_small_tools").
6. No model returned a 0–1 fraction this round (raw `deductible_percent` values: 0, 30, 40, 50, 70, 80, 100, null), so the normalizer had nothing to correct; gpt-5.4-mini proposed 100 where 40 was saved once → the by-design percent-mismatch rejection. Rule-4 personal calls (Starbucks, Netflix, groceries, Planet Fitness, Coinbase, Coursera new trade) came back `ok`/nondeductible (kind personal or expense) and are counted as status disagreements against pre-rule labels; none was approved. Stripe payout labeled TRANSFER_IN by Plaid was called a transfer by gpt-4o-mini/gpt-4o — income that would leave the totals; gpt-4.1-mini/gpt-5.4-mini asked the source.
7. Recommendation for `transaction`: **gpt-4.1-mini**. Best status agreement (61/73 corpus, 63/73 descriptors), 0 rejections, most approvals (12 corpus, 26 descriptor), fastest (p50 2.3 s / p95 2.7 s), second only to gpt-4o on corpus category agreement (55 vs 57 of 73), $0.0011 per transaction (2.4× gpt-4o-mini, 1/7 of gpt-4o, 1/2 of gpt-5.4-mini).
