## 8. OBBBA "no tax on tips," "no tax on overtime," car loan interest deductions

All three: available to itemizers and non-itemizers (§63(b)(5)-(7)); tax years beginning after Dec. 31, 2024 and before Jan. 1, 2029 (2025–2028); dollar amounts and MAGI thresholds are **not indexed**, so 2027 figures equal 2025/2026 figures; MAGI = AGI + §§911/931/933 exclusions; claimed on new Schedule 1-A (Form 1040). Sources: PL119-21 §§70201–70203; FS-2025-03; WFTC-IND — VERIFIED.

### 8a. Qualified tips deduction (new §224)

| Parameter | Value (2025–2028) |
|---|---|
| Maximum deduction | $25,000 per return (not per spouse / per occupation — TD10044 preamble §11) |
| MAGI phaseout | Reduced by $100 for each $1,000 by which MAGI exceeds $150,000 ($300,000 joint). Fully phased out at $400,000 single / $550,000 MFJ (COMPUTED) |
| Qualified tips | Voluntary cash/charged/electronic tips (incl. tip-pool shares for employees), not negotiated, determined by the payor; received in an occupation on Treasury's List of Occupations that Receive Tips (TTOC codes 100s–800s; >70 occupations) — TD10044 / IR-2026-49 |
| Excluded | Tips received in a §199A(d)(2) specified service trade or business (SSTB); an employee is treated as in an SSTB if the employer's trade or business is an SSTB (§224(d)(2)(B)). Mandatory service charges are not tips unless the customer can disregard/modify them (TD10044) |
| SSTB transition relief | Under N2025-69, until Jan. 1 of the year after final regs on the SSTB determination are issued, IRS treats employees and self-employed in a listed occupation as not in an SSTB. TD10044 **did not finalize** SSTB rules (§1.224-1(g) reserved), so this relief remains in effect through at least 2026 |
| Reporting | Deduction limited to tips shown on W-2 (§6051(a)(18)), 1099-NEC/MISC (§6041(d)(3), §6041A(e)(3)), 1099-K (§6050W(f)(2)), or Form 4137. For 2025, payors were not required to separately state tips (Notice 2025-62 penalty relief); N2025-69 lets recipients substantiate with POS reports/tip logs if tips are included in the 1099 total. Separate reporting required from 2026 |
| SSN / filing status | Work-eligible SSN required; married must file jointly |
| **Self-employed nuance** (§224(c); Reg. §1.224-1(e)) | For a non-employee trade or business, deductible tips cannot exceed gross income from that trade or business (incl. the tips) minus deductions allocable to it (i.e., Schedule C net profit before the §224 deduction). The deduction cannot create/increase a loss. Whether SE health insurance, half SE tax, and SE retirement deductions are "allocable" was left open by TD10044. Self-employed in an SSTB are ineligible (subject to the transition relief above). Statements issued to a sole proprietorship / disregarded SMLLC are treated as issued to the owner. Tips deducted under §224 are excluded from QBI (§199A(c)(4)(D)) |
| Interaction with overtime | Qualified overtime excludes any qualified tip (§225(c)(2)) |

### 8b. Qualified overtime compensation deduction (new §225)

| Parameter | Value (2025–2028) |
|---|---|
| Maximum deduction | $12,500 ($25,000 joint return) |
| MAGI phaseout | $100 per $1,000 of MAGI over $150,000 ($300,000 joint). Fully phased out at $275,000 single / $550,000 MFJ (COMPUTED) |
| Qualified overtime | Only the premium portion (e.g., the "half" of time-and-a-half) of overtime **required by FLSA §7** in excess of the regular rate. Overtime paid under state law, contract, or CBA beyond the FLSA requirement does not qualify; FLSA-exempt workers have no qualified overtime (FS-2026-13 Q1) |
| Reporting | Must appear on W-2 box 12 code TT, 1099-NEC box 1d, or 1099-MISC box 14 from 2026 (FS-2026-13 Q6). For 2025, Notice 2025-62 relief; individuals may use N2025-69 methods |
| SSN / filing status | Work-eligible SSN; married must file jointly |
| Withholding | Employers may not reduce withholding for the deduction absent a new W-4 (FS-2026-13 Q8-Q9) |
| **Self-employed nuance** | A true independent contractor generally has **no** qualified overtime, because FLSA §7 overtime applies only to employees. The sole 1099 case is a worker who is an employee for FLSA purposes but treated as an independent contractor for tax purposes (FS-2026-13 Q7: "rare") |

### 8c. Qualified passenger vehicle loan interest (§163(h)(4), PL119-21 §70203)

| Parameter | Value (2025–2028) |
|---|---|
| Maximum interest taken into account | $10,000 per year |
| MAGI phaseout | Reduced by $200 for each $1,000 **(or portion thereof)** of MAGI over $100,000 ($200,000 joint). Fully phased out at $150,000 / $250,000 (COMPUTED) |

MFS filers are not barred (no joint-return requirement in §70203), but the joint threshold is $200,000 and the single/MFS threshold $100,000 |
