# 2027 federal tax readiness: source ledger

Reviewed **September 23, 2026**. Scope: federal individual/sole-proprietor planning and transaction guidance. This is a bounded source review, not every IRC section, a complete return specification, or a certification of state-tax support. **Tax year 2027 is generally filed in 2028; the 2027 filing season predominantly concerns tax year 2026.**

Employer contributions, monthly eligibility, Medicare enrollment, family allocation and last-month testing rules still apply. Bronze/catastrophic coverage and qualifying direct-primary-care arrangements have special rules effective from 2026. | Household income as federal poverty percentage | Initial % | Final % |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Below 133% | 2.15 | 2.15 |
| 133% to below 150% | 3.23 | 4.30 |
| 150% to below 200% | 4.30 | 6.78 |
| 200% to below 250% | 6.78 | 8.66 |
| 250% to below 300% | 8.66 | 10.22 |
| 300% through 400% | 10.22 | 10.22 |

The table alone is insufficient for a credit calculation: eligibility, household composition, monthly coverage, benchmark premiums, employer affordability, advance payments and the applicable poverty guideline all matter. [IRS PTC questions and answers](https://www.irs.gov/affordable-care-act/individuals-and-families/questions-and-answers-on-the-premium-tax-credit). Do not interpret the first table row as automatic eligibility below the poverty line or use this table to enable a full Form 8962 module.

## Enacted rules continuing into, or starting in, 2027

| Topic | Established rule and implementation boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Temporary tips deduction | Through 2028: up to $25,000 per return; MAGI phaseout begins $150,000 / $300,000 joint. Reported qualifying tips and eligible occupation required; business-net-income limit for self-employed taxpayers. |
| Temporary overtime deduction | Through 2028: up to $12,500 / $25,000 joint; same MAGI phaseout start. Only qualifying FLSA premium compensation, not all overtime wages or a sole proprietor's extra hours. |
| Temporary senior deduction | Through 2028: up to $6,000 per eligible age-65 person; MAGI phaseout begins $75,000 / $150,000 joint. Separate from the aged standard deduction. |
| Temporary vehicle-loan-interest deduction | Through 2028: up to $10,000; MAGI phaseout begins $100,000 / $200,000 joint. Requires qualifying new personal-use vehicle, post-2024 secured purchase loan, U.S. final assembly and VIN; not lease payments. |

These deductions require their complete eligibility and filing conditions; tips/overtime do not eliminate payroll or self-employment taxes. [IRS individual and worker provisions](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/working-families-tax-cuts-individuals-and-workers).

**Tips guidance nuance:** The April 2026 final regulations finalized the occupation/qualified-tip rules but reserved the SSTB exclusion for separate guidance. Notice 2025-69's transition relief ends January 1 following final SSTB regulations. Do not infer that an April 2026 final occupation rule by itself ended SSTB relief for 2027. Recheck the separate SSTB rule before enabling a definitive 2027 exclusion. Mandatory service charges are not voluntary qualified tips. [2026-18 IRB, final regulations and SSTB discussion](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2026-18_IRB).

**SALT:** The 2027 cap is $40,804 ($20,402 MFS), corroborated by the [IRS Internal Revenue Manual](https://www.irs.gov/irm/part21/irm_21-006-004r). The enacted 101% formula produces MAGI threshold $510,050 ($255,025 MFS). General formula: `max(10000, 40804 - 0.30 * max(0, MAGI - 510050))`. MFS formula: `0.5 * max(10000, 40804 - 0.30 * max(0, MAGI - 255025))`. Actual eligible taxes still limit the deduction. [P.L. 119-21 §70120](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ21/pdf/PLAW-119publ21.pdf). These are statutory calculations, not estimated CPI adjustments.

**Charity:** From 2026, qualifying cash gifts by nonitemizers have a $1,000 / $2,000 joint limit; gifts to individuals do not qualify, and substantiation remains necessary. [IRS Topic 506](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc506). Itemized charitable deductions have a 0.5% contribution-base floor, with carryforward and other percentage-limit interactions. [Publication 505, charitable deduction worksheet](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p505). Do not classify personal charity as a Schedule C business expense.

**Scholarship-contribution credit (§25F):** Starts January 1, 2027; maximum $1,700 for qualifying cash contributions to qualifying scholarship-granting organizations. State advance election alone is not proof that a recipient qualifies. The state must supply its organization list. [IRS FSTC program and participation status](https://www.irs.gov/government-entities/federal-state-local-governments/federal-scholarship-tax-credit-fstc). The credit is nonrefundable, generally permits five-year carryforward, is reduced for allowed state credits and cannot duplicate a charitable deduction. [Notice 2025-70](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2025-50_IRB). Require eligibility review rather than crediting a bank transaction automatically.

**Saver's Match (§6433):** For 2027 contributions, up to 50% of the first $2,000 per person can become a $1,000 government retirement-account contribution, generally claimed in 2028. The match replaces the retirement-contribution Saver's Credit; ABLE contributions remain distinct. Published 2027 full-match / zero-match MAGI thresholds: single or MFS $20,500 / $35,500; HOH $30,750 / $53,250; MFJ or QSS $41,000 / $71,000. Age, student/dependent status, residence and contribution eligibility matter. [IRS Saver's Match](https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/savers-match). Notice 2026-48 describes anticipated regulations, withdrawal adjustments, MAGI addbacks and percentage-point rounding; it is not a completed filing implementation. [Notice 2026-48](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2026-35_IRB).

**Business deductions:** The 100% bonus-depreciation framework for qualifying property acquired after January 19, 2025 continues; §179's annual dollar caps remain indexed. Assets still need dates, business use, basis, election and recapture review. [IRS business provisions](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/working-families-tax-cuts-businesses). The permanent QBI framework remains, while its annual 2027 thresholds/minimum amounts need the actual annual release. [IRS Publication 334](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p334).

**Reporting:** The §§6041/6041A $2,000 base applies to 2026 payments; inflation adjustment begins for payments in calendar years after 2026. Do not treat that base as a verified 2027 annual threshold. [2026-19 IRB, reporting-rule background](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2026-19_IRB). Third-party network 1099-K reporting generally requires both more than $20,000 and more than 200 transactions; receiving no form does not make income tax-free. [IRS information-return instructions](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p1099).

**Expired incentives:** New vehicle acquisitions in 2027 do not receive the former §§30D/25E/45W credits; §§25C/25D residential benefits also have pre-2027 cutoffs. Relevant acquisition/expenditure dates and any earlier qualifying events must be checked. [IRS energy-credit expiration summary](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/working-families-tax-cuts).

## Annual values still unavailable at this review

No official final 2027 values were located in the reviewed IRS/SSA annual releases for these items. This is a dated research result, not a prediction about later publication:

- Ordinary/capital-gains bracket dollar boundaries; basic/aged/blind/dependent standard deduction; AMT; EITC; CTC/ACTC inflation amounts; QBI thresholds and indexed minimum; §179 and heavy-SUV limits; excess-business-loss limit and other annual inflation items. Latest comprehensive release reviewed: [Rev. Proc. 2025-32 for 2026](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2025-45_IRB).
- Social Security taxable wage base. The [SSA actual base table](https://www.ssa.gov/oact/COLA/cbb.html) currently ends in 2026; trustees' future-year projections are not the final determination.
- IRA/401(k)/SEP/SIMPLE contribution and indexed eligibility limits. Current [IRS retirement contribution guidance](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-contributions) and [IRA limit page](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-ira-contribution-limits) provide 2026 values.
- Business/medical mileage and vehicle depreciation tables. Current [IRS mileage table](https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/standard-mileage-rates) provides 2026, not 2027.
- The adjusted calendar-2027 business-payee reporting threshold; final 2027 forms/instructions and complete state conformity/annual tables are separate release dependencies.

Keep these values absent/pending, not zero or copied from 2026. Published individual rules can power education and bounded planning while full 2027 federal/state estimates and filing remain gated on missing inputs, annual releases and implementation coverage.

## Adjacent 2026 correction identified

The current [IRS mileage table](https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/standard-mileage-rates) now distinguishes January–June 2026 business **72.5¢** / medical **20.5¢** from July–December business **76¢** / medical **23.5¢**. Charitable mileage remains **14¢**. A single 72.5¢ constant for all 2026 business trips is insufficient. Preserve trip dates and apply the effective-date rate; this does not establish any 2027 rate.

## Implementation discipline

1. Version sources, review date, tax year, effective dates and publication status.
2. Keep enacted fixed rules separate from annual published figures and implementation support.
3. Preserve missing-fact prompts and user confirmation; a merchant/photo alone proves neither business purpose nor eligibility.
4. Keep education distinct from a complete calculation, return-ready export or filing transmission.
5. Update the dated ledger when official releases arrive; never silently extrapolate to satisfy a year selector.
