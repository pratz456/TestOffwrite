2. **P1 (prompt + gate) — solo coffee is still asked.** `starbucks-alone-personal` ("My morning coffee") and the relabelled `starbucks-alone-over-eager` ("Coffee before work") get "Who was at this meal and what business was discussed?" from gpt-4.1-mini in 2/2 runs (and `starbucks-alone-over-eager` from gpt-5.4-mini) although the raw prose itself says the coffee is personal; `uber-personal-ride` ("Ride home from a concert") regressed from ok/personal in every round-4 run to the merchant's "Where did this ride go?" question in both runs. `EXPLICIT_PERSONAL_NOTE` already matches "my morning coffee" and "coffee before work" but the gate runs only when `is_deductible === true` (`lib/ai/transaction-tax-policy.ts:510`), so a model that asks instead of approving is never corrected. Fix: apply `markPersonal` whenever `kind === 'expense'`, the saved note matches the pattern and the answer is not already personal — approvals and questions alike — and add "ride home from a concert"-style leisure wording to the Rule 4 examples.
3. **P1 (gate) — personal denials with a §162 citation become a generic business-purpose question.** gpt-4.1-mini phrases correct denials as `status ok, transaction_kind expense, expense_type personal, is_deductible false, evidence_ids [business-162, records-334]` (desc `parking-commute` "Commuting parking is a personal expense", `city-parking-ticket` "Parking tickets are fines and penalties", `home-dishwasher-repair` "This is a personal home repair", r2 desc `home-repair`). Fix: for `is_deductible === false` answers, accept `business-162`/`records-334` as a valid denial citation (or swap in `personal-262` instead of raising the off-category flag) and evaluate the commuting, penalty, personal-legal-matter, personal-coverage and home-repair readings on denials too, so `parking-commute` lands on `markPersonal` and `city-parking-ticket` on the `expense_review` fine question the corpus expects.
